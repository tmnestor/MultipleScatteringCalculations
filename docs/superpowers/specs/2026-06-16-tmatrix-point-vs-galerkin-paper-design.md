# Design Spec — Standalone Paper: Point-Source vs Body-Force Galerkin T-Matrix

**Date:** 2026-06-16
**Status:** Design (awaiting user review → writing-plans)
**Type:** Research-paper synthesis (lualatex), new `paper/` directory

---

## 1. Goal

Produce one **self-contained, standalone research paper** (lualatex) that synthesises the
last several months of learnings on the single-site elastic cubic-scatterer T-matrix into a
single coherent argument. The paper's axis is a **hierarchy-of-approximations comparison**:

> What does the body-force **Galerkin bilinear** closure give over a **local point-source**
> representation of an elastic cubic scatterer — *if anything*?

The verdict is **derived from a reproducible evidence table**, not asserted up front (per the
"re-derive from evidence first" decision). The evidence is generated by **re-running** the
project's validation scripts/tests against the exact oracles (elastic Mie sphere, Kennett
layered reflectivity) so every number and figure in the paper is reproducible.

### Hard editorial rule — T₂₇ is excluded
The 27-component Galerkin extension is a **measured failure** (18 quadratic modes give no
Mie-consistent single-site far-field gain; inter-voxel T₂₇ coupling ≤ 0.07%). It is **not**
presented-then-dismissed. It appears **only** as: (a) one scoping sentence in the
introduction, and (b) the top, excluded rung of the hierarchy ladder with a one-line pointer
to the measured ≤0.07% inter-voxel result. No T₂₇ derivation, no T₂₇ tables, no T₂₇ figures.
All harvested content currently framed around T₂₇ must be **structurally reduced to the T₉
subspace** (constants + symmetric-linear polynomials), not merely trimmed.

### Audience / format
Standalone research paper: tight, argument-driven. Abstract → intro → self-contained
background → method → evidence → conclusion → references → appendices. Background theory is
included to make the paper self-contained (elastic Mie sphere, Kennett, the point→Eshelby→T₉
hierarchy), but it is not exhaustive textbook material.

### References discipline
Every claim is flagged **standard** (cite the seismic/micromechanics literature) or **novel
(this work)**. The reference list is split accordingly.

---

## 2. Non-goals (YAGNI)

- No T₂₇ content beyond the single scoping sentence + excluded ladder rung.
- No new physics derivations beyond writing up what the code already implements and validates.
- No plotting library beyond TikZ/pgfplots (consistent with existing docs).
- No edits to the existing `docs/*.tex` or `LatexPDFs/*.tex` sources — this is a *new* document
  that harvests *from* them. (Originals stay as-is.)
- Not a thesis chapter and not an internal report — a submittable-style manuscript.

- Coordinate system per CLAUDE.md: z = axis 0 (down), x = axis 1, y = axis 2; Voigt order
  (ε11, ε22, ε33, 2ε23, 2ε13, 2ε12).

**Sweep axes for the master cost–accuracy table (§ paper-7):** contrast {weak, moderate,
strong −60% Δρ} × ka {0.05, 0.1, 0.3, 0.5} × polarization {P, SV, SH} × representation

**Acceptance for Phase 0:** `run_evidence.py` completes in env `seismic`; CSVs committed;
numbers are internally consistent with the committed test assertions (any divergence from
memory/commit values is investigated and the *measured* number wins).

## 5. Paper section design (Approach A — hierarchy of approximations)

1. **Abstract** — the question + the evidence-derived verdict in one paragraph.
2. **Introduction** — elastodynamic VIE problem; the point-vs-Galerkin question; explicit
   contributions list flagged standard vs novel; one sentence scoping out T₂₇ with a pointer.
3. **Background theory (self-contained)** — harvest + de-T₂₇ from `point_vs_volume_tmatrix_notes.tex`,
   `cube_galerkin27.tex §1`, `mie_multipole_derivation.tex`:
   - 3.1 Elastodynamic Lippmann–Schwinger / volume integral equation.
   - 3.2 The exact oracles: finite-radius finite-contrast **elastic Mie sphere**; **Kennett**
     layered reflectivity.
   - 3.3 The single-site hierarchy: point/Born → Eshelby-point → volume-averaged body-force
     Galerkin T₉.
4. **The body-force Galerkin closure (T₉)** — harvest from `cube_galerkin27.tex §2–5` +
   `cube_tmatrix_closedform.tex`, **reduced to the T₉ subspace**: bilinear forms, CM/relative
   reduction + cube-autocorrelation tent, master integrals (only those needed for T₉),
   closed-form effective contrasts Δλ\*, Δμ\*, Δρ\*.
5. **Single-site self-energy corrections (the rungs that pay off)** —
   - 5.1 Finite-size form factor (cube −1/3), Kennett-validated.
   - 5.2 Self-consistent Eshelby amplification `A_u = 1/(1 − ω²Δρ Γ₀)`.
   - 5.3 **Radiation reaction = local density of states (LDOS) at the origin** — the content
     specifically requested. Density: `Im[Γ₀] = ω/(12πρ)(1/α³ + 2/β³) = (1/3)S_α + (2/3)S_β`.
     Modulus: `Im[Δc*]` as the **strain LDOS** — moment dipole `M = V·Δc:ε` radiating with
     Aki–Richards power `P_rad = ω⁴/(8πρα⁵)⟨(r̂·M·r̂)²⟩ + ω⁴/(8πρβ⁵)⟨|(M·r̂)_⊥|²⟩`, giving
     `Im[Δλ*] = -(2V/ω)[c_P/15(15Δλ²+20ΔλΔμ+4Δμ²) − c_S/15·4Δμ²]`,
     `Im[Δμ*] = -(V/ω)[c_P/15·8Δμ² + c_S/15·12Δμ²]`. Derived from the background Green's
     function (NOT by imposing σ_ext=σ_sc); optical theorem closes to 1.0 as a *consequence*;
     gated against exact Mie a₀/a₂ (correct sign, no cross-channel leakage). Harvest from
     `mie_finite_contrast_validation.tex`; derivation matched line-for-line to
     `effective_contrasts.py::_modulus_radiation_reaction` / `_compute_phi_diagonal`.
6. **Inter-site coupling: where the point representation suffices** — harvest from
   `inter_voxel_propagator.tex` + `slab_scattering_explanation.tex`: point vs volume-averaged
   propagator; the vertical-coupling-vs-transverse-phase argument (volume averaging corrects
   vertical coupling, not the ~1 rad/cell transverse phase); the ≤0.07% T₂₇ inter-voxel
   result (excluded-rung evidence); slab→Kennett ~1% floor.
7. **Evidence synthesis & cost–accuracy** — the master table (error vs ka × contrast ×
   polarization × representation, with cost proxy); the derived statement of *where on the
   ladder the error stops dropping faster than the complexity rises*.
8. **Discussion / Conclusion** — the verdict as an explicit output of §7: single-site
   self-energy rewards the volume/Eshelby treatment (form factor, amplification, LDOS
   radiation reaction); inter-site coupling does not reward it beyond a local point
   propagator; T₂₇ is never repaid.
9. **References** — split bib (see §7 below).
10. **Appendices** — master integrals + high-precision closed forms (harvest from
    `cube_galerkin27_results.tex`, `cube_galerkin27_closedforms.tex`), T₉-relevant subset only.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

1. `paper/tmatrix_point_vs_galerkin.tex` compiles cleanly under lualatex in-place (two passes
   + reference pass), zero unresolved references/citations.
2. **Zero** T₂₇ derivation/table/figure content; T₂₇ appears only as the scoping sentence +
   excluded ladder rung with the ≤0.07% pointer.
3. Every quantitative claim traces to a committed `evidence/*.csv` artefact produced by
   `run_evidence.py`, and the verdict in §8(paper) follows from the §7(paper) master table.
4. Every figure F1–F6 renders; F1 and F6 reflect the re-run evidence numbers.
5. Every citation is flagged standard vs novel and resolves in `references.bib`.
6. The originals in `docs/` and `LatexPDFs/` are unmodified.

---

## 10. Risks / open items

- **Evidence divergence:** a re-run number may disagree with memory/commit text. Rule: the
  measured number wins; investigate and footnote if material.
- **Citation verification:** elastic-Mie and DDA-convergence citations must be checked for
  exact authorship/year before inclusion (no fabricated references).
- **T₉ reduction fidelity:** harvested T₂₇ derivations must be checked that the 9-subspace
  reduction is physically the constants+symmetric-linear block (no orphaned quadratic terms).
